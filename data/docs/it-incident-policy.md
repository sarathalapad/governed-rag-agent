# IT Incident Management Policy

Acme Financial (fictional company) - sample document for this demo.

## Severity levels

P1 (critical): a customer-facing service is down or a security breach is suspected. Response within 15 minutes, 24x7. The on-call incident commander must be paged immediately.

P2 (high): a service is degraded or an internal system used by more than 50 employees is unavailable. Response within 1 hour during business hours.

P3 (normal): a single user is affected or a workaround exists. Response within 1 business day.

## Reporting an incident

Employees report incidents through the IT service portal or by calling the service desk on extension 4400. Security incidents must also be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of discovery.

## Post-incident review

Every P1 incident requires a blameless post-incident review within 5 business days. The review records the timeline, root cause, customer impact and follow-up actions with owners and due dates.
