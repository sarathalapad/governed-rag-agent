# Leave Policy

Acme Financial (fictional company) - sample document for this demo.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Other parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting leave

Leave is requested in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies. The line manager approves or declines within 3 business days.
